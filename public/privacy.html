Privacy Policy for NutriFit AI

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

NutriFit AI ("the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our client-side Progressive Web Application (PWA) handles your data.

1. Local & Client-Side Data Storage

NutriFit AI operates primarily as a client-side application. All personal data, including your profile, food photos, meal descriptions, weight logs, and daily targets, is stored locally on your device via your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage, or in your personal Google Drive account if you select that option.

2. Google Fit & Google User Data

When you choose to connect Google Fit, NutriFit AI requests read-only access to your physical activity and body metrics (fitness.activity.read and fitness.body.read) solely to calculate and display your daily calories burned (BMR + exercise).

  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your Google Fit data is never sent to any external server or third party.
  • No Commercial Use: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your fitness or dietary data.
  • Revocation: You can disconnect Google Fit at any time inside the Application settings or via your Google Account Security dashboard.

3. AI Processing (Google Gemini API)

When you use AI photo or voice recognition to log meals, the image or description is sent directly from your browser to the Google Gemini API using your provided API key to estimate nutritional information and macros.

4. Contact Information
